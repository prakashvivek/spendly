Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 6, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register. We also collect the expense records you create, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you choose to log.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate the Service — to authenticate your account, store and display your expenses, and generate the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app. We do not use your expense data for advertising, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your data will be permanently removed from our systems.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third-party advertisers. We may rely on trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting and email delivery services) to operate the Service, and these providers only process data as needed to support that infrastructure.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website.
